Why Engram Exists

Traditional payment gateways and aggregators frequently shut down or freeze merchant accounts in specialized verticals with little explanation. When a business is labeled "high-risk", it often faces lengthy delays, sudden rolling reserve holds, and abrupt termination notices.

Engram was created to eliminate this fragility. We provide the software layer and acquiring network that high-risk merchants need to process payments reliably.

By matching merchants directly with processors that understand their business model and connecting multiple providers into a single smart routing layer, Engram ensures payments stay live, disputes are managed proactively, and businesses can scale with confidence.
