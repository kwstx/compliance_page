01. Overview & Scope

At Engram, Inc. ("Engram", "we", "us", or "our"), protecting your privacy and maintaining the confidentiality of sensitive financial and personal data is central to our mission. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, disclose, and safeguard information when you access our website, use our payment orchestration platform, integrate our APIs, or interact with our merchant services (collectively, the "Services").

Depending on your relationship with Engram, we may act as:

  • Data Controller: With respect to merchant account profile data, business contact information, underwriting records, billing details, and direct communications with us.
  • Data Processor / Service Provider: When processing, routing, and tokenizing end-customer payment transactions on behalf of our merchants who use our orchestration gateway.
Scope Note: If you are a customer purchasing goods or services from a merchant utilizing Engram’s orchestration engine, the merchant is the primary controller of your personal data. Please refer directly to the merchant's privacy policy for details on their data handling practices.

02. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver robust payment orchestration, underwriting, fraud mitigation, and compliance services:

A. Merchant & Business Information

When merchants register or apply for Engram processing services, we collect:

  • Corporate Identity: Legal business name, DBA, corporate registration number, tax identification number (EIN/VAT), jurisdiction, registered address, and website URL.
  • Authorized Representatives & Beneficial Owners: Name, business email address, phone number, date of birth, residential address, government ID (passport or driver's license), and ownership percentages for individuals owning 25% or more equity.
  • Financial & Settlement Data: Corporate bank account numbers, routing numbers, processing history statements, and settlement preference details.

B. Transaction & Payment Data

When transactions are routed through our smart gateway:

  • Tokenized Card Information: Cardholder name, card brand, first 6 and last 4 digits of the card (BIN/last 4), expiration date, and irreversible tokenized identifiers. Full Primary Account Numbers (PANs) and CVVs are handled exclusively within PCI DSS Level 1 compliant secure tokenization vaults and are never exposed in plaintext.
  • Transaction Metadata: Transaction amount, currency, timestamp, merchant order ID, IP address, billing address, shipping country, and acquirer response codes.
  • Risk & Anti-Fraud Signals: Device fingerprint data, velocity indicators, chargeback dispute notifications, and fraud scoring attributes.

C. Automated & Technical Data

When interacting with our website and merchant dashboard:

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, operating system, referrer URL, pages visited, timestamps).
  • API usage telemetry, latency metrics, and error diagnostics.
  • Essential cookies and authentication tokens.

03. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data strictly for lawful operational, regulatory, and business purposes, including:

  • Payment Orchestration & Routing: Dynamically routing transactions to optimal acquiring banks, managing automated failovers, and maximizing authorization rates.
  • Underwriting & Compliance Verification: Performing mandatory Know Your Business (KYB), Know Your Customer (KYC), anti-money laundering (AML), and sanctions screening checks.
  • Fraud Prevention & Risk Scoring: Detecting anomalous transaction spikes, preventing chargebacks, evaluating chargeback alerts, and maintaining network integrity.
  • Settlement & Reconciliation: Calculating interchange, platform fees, rolling reserve balances, and generating merchant settlement reports.
  • Customer Support & API Operations: Responding to merchant support inquiries, resolving routing issues, and maintaining uptime across our developer APIs.
  • Legal Compliance: Satisfying financial record-keeping mandates, responding to subpoenas, and cooperating with acquiring banks and regulatory authorities.

04. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR/UK GDPR)

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK Data Protection Act, we process personal data under the following legal grounds:

  • Contractual Performance: Processing necessary to deliver the Services under our Terms of Service.
  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with statutory obligations such as AML/KYC regulations, tax laws, and payment network mandates.
  • Legitimate Interests: Securing our infrastructure, preventing payment fraud, optimizing gateway routing, and improving platform reliability.
  • Consent: Where explicitly provided, such as for non-essential cookies or direct marketing communications.

05. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We disclose data solely to authorized third parties under strict confidentiality and security commitments:

  • Acquiring Banks & Payment Processors: Member banks, PSPs, and card networks (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) to process and settle transactions.
  • Identity & Risk Verification Partners: Specialized vendors providing KYB/KYC verification, OFAC screening, and real-time chargeback alert feeds (e.g., Ethoca, Verifi).
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Secure, SOC 2 and PCI DSS certified cloud hosting and vault facilities (e.g., AWS) located in secure data centers.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Law enforcement, financial regulators, or courts when required by law or valid legal process.
  • Corporate Transactions: In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset sale, subject to customary confidentiality agreements.

06. Security & PCI DSS Standards

Security is the foundation of Engram's infrastructure. We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures:

  • PCI DSS Level 1 Certified: Our environment undergoes annual external Qualified Security Assessor (QSA) audits and continuous vulnerability scans.
  • Encryption & Vaulting: Sensitive cardholder data is tokenized immediately. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and data at rest is protected with AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Controls & MFA: Strict principle of least privilege, hardware-backed multi-factor authentication, and comprehensive immutable audit logging.
  • DDoS & Web Application Firewalls: Continuous edge protection and rate limiting to defend against malicious traffic and brute-force attacks.

07. Data Retention

We retain personal and transaction data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, satisfy legal and regulatory requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Due to financial regulatory, tax, and anti-money laundering requirements, transaction logs and merchant identity verification documentation are typically retained for seven (7) years following account termination. Non-essential operational logs are automatically purged or anonymized on shorter, recurring schedules.

08. International Data Transfers

Engram operates globally. Personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our cloud infrastructure and service providers operate.

When transferring data from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we utilize approved transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and relevant adequacy decisions to ensure that personal data remains protected to equivalent legal standards.

09. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific statutory rights regarding your personal information:

Your Rights Under GDPR & International Laws

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data, subject to statutory financial retention mandates.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: Object to or restrict specific processing operations.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

Your Rights Under the CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)

  • Right to Know: Disclose categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, sold, or shared.
  • Right to Delete & Correct: Request deletion or correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: Engram does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, please submit your request through our Contact Form. We will verify and respond to your request within the statutory timeframe.

10. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to ensure seamless platform operation and security:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Required for secure login, session authentication, and CSRF protection in the merchant portal.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Assist us in understanding how visitors interact with our documentation, landing pages, and APIs to improve performance.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your dashboard display configurations and language preferences.

You may configure your browser settings to reject or delete non-essential cookies. However, disabling essential cookies may impact the functionality of the merchant portal.

11. Children's Privacy

Our Services are strictly designed for commercial entities, merchants, and individuals aged eighteen (18) and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that an individual under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in regulatory standards, operational enhancements, or new platform features. When updates occur, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

For material changes, we will provide advance notice via email to registered merchants or through an in-portal notification prior to the change becoming effective.

13. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions, inquiries, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or our data protection practices, please contact our team directly through our Contact Form.
