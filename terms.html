02. Description of Services

BloomFi provides a specialized payment orchestration and gateway routing platform designed to maintain payment continuity, optimize acceptance rates, and protect revenue for high-risk, high-volume, and subscription businesses.

Our platform includes the following capabilities:

  • Multi-Processor Routing: Smart dynamic routing of credit card, debit, ACH, crypto, and alternative payment transactions across multiple acquiring partners.
  • Processor Redundancy & Failover: Automated failover mechanisms to redirect transactions in real time in the event of provider downtime or sudden account restrictions.
  • Risk & Chargeback Monitoring: Continuous transaction monitoring, predictive chargeback alerts, fraud prevention tools, and velocity checks.
  • Unified Merchant Dashboard & API: A single integration point to manage transactions, settlements, refunds, and processor relationships.
