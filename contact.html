Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our onboarding, underwriting, and gateway integrations.

How fast can high-risk merchants get onboarded?

Our expedited underwriting network typically reviews applications within 24 to 48 business hours once corporate documentation and processing history are submitted.

Can we connect BloomFi to our existing gateway or CRM?

Yes. BloomFi offers universal RESTful APIs, webhooks, and pre-built connectors for Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, and custom billing platforms.

What documentation is required to start underwriting?

Standard requirements include Certificate of Incorporation, government photo ID for beneficial owners, recent 3–6 months processing statements, and voided corporate check.

How does BloomFi prevent sudden payment shutdowns?

We distribute transaction volume across multiple merchant IDs (MIDs) and backup acquirers. If one processor declines or flags traffic, our engine automatically routes to a secondary healthy provider without disruption.
